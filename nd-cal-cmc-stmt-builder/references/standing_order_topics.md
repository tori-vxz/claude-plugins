# Standing Order Topics — N.D. Cal. CMS Structure and Formatting Baseline

## 19-Topic Structure

The N.D. Cal. Standing Order for All Judges mandates 19 topics for Joint Case Management Statements. Each topic is either **joint** (single unified section) or **split** (separate "Plaintiffs' Position" and "Defendant's Position" subsections).

| Topic | Subject | Format |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Jurisdiction, Service, and Venue | Split |
| 2 | Parties and Related Cases | Joint |
| 3 | Factual Background | Split |
| 4 | Legal Theories | Split |
| 5 | Relief Sought and Prayer for Relief | Joint |
| 6 | Evidence Preservation | Joint |
| 7 | Disclosures and Initial Disclosures Deadline | Joint |
| 8 | Discovery | Joint |
| 9 | Class Actions / Subclasses | Joint |
| 10 | Related Cases | Joint |
| 11 | Relief | Split |
| 12 | Settlement and ADR | Joint |
| 13 | Other References | Joint |
| 14 | Narrowing of Issues | Joint |
| 15 | Scheduling | Joint + Table |
| 16 | Trial | Joint |
| 17 | Non-Party Entities | Joint |
| 18 | Professional Conduct | Joint |
| 19 | Other Matters | Joint |

## Standard Language Blocks

The following 13 topics use verbatim standard language in every CMS draft (with pluralization adjustments for Plaintiff/Plaintiffs and Defendant/Defendants):

### Topic 6: Evidence Preservation
"The parties shall preserve all documents, electronically stored information ('ESI'), and tangible things in the possession, custody, or control of each party that are relevant to the subject matter of this litigation. Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(f), the parties have met and conferred regarding preservation obligations. Each party shall notify the other parties immediately upon discovery of the loss or destruction of any such evidence."

### Topic 7: Disclosures and Initial Disclosures Deadline
"Initial disclosures shall be made [XX calendar days] after the parties' Rule 26(f) conference (as calculated under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 6(a)), or by [DATE], whichever is earlier. The parties shall supplement their disclosures as required by Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(e)."

(Note: The deadline is 14 days after the 26(f) conference, FRCP-6-adjusted. Source this from the matter's calendar lookup.)

### Topic 8: Discovery
"The parties shall conduct discovery in accordance with the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and the applicable local rules for the Northern District of California. [PLAINTIFF/DEFENDANT POSITION ON SCOPE OF DISCOVERY, ANTICIPATED TIMING, ESI PROTOCOLS]."

### Topic 9: Class Actions (if applicable)
"This matter involves a putative class action. [DESCRIPTION OF PROPOSED CLASS DEFINITION, NUMEROSITY, COMMONALITY, TYPICALITY, ADEQUACY OF REPRESENTATION]. The parties shall comply with Civil L.R. 16-9(b) regarding class action supplemental requirements."

### Topic 10: Related Cases
"The following related cases have been identified: [LIST RELATED CASES BY CAPTION, CASE NUMBER, COURT, AND JUDGE]. The parties shall promptly notify the Court of any additional related cases discovered during the pendency of this action."

### Topic 12: Settlement and ADR
"The parties shall explore settlement and alternative dispute resolution opportunities as appropriate. Absent waiver or agreement otherwise, the parties shall participate in a mediation conference prior to the discovery cutoff date."

### Topic 13: Other References
"The parties acknowledge that this Case Management Statement is made pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(f) and the Court's Standing Order for All Judges. [ANY ADDITIONAL REFERENCES, PRIOR ORDERS, OR APPLICABLE PROCEDURAL RULES]."

### Topic 14: Narrowing of Issues
"[DISCUSS ANY ANTICIPATED STIPULATIONS, NARROWING OF LEGAL OR FACTUAL ISSUES, OR BIFURCATION OF CLAIMS FOR PURPOSES OF TRIAL]."

### Topic 16: Trial
"The parties anticipate [PLAINTIFF/DEFENDANT ESTIMATE] that this case will be tried [JURY / BENCH TRIAL]. [PLAINTIFF/DEFENDANT POSITION ON ESTIMATED TRIAL LENGTH]."

### Topic 17: Non-Party Entities
"[IDENTIFY ANY NON-PARTY ENTITIES THAT MAY BE AFFECTED BY OR INTERESTED IN THE OUTCOME OF THIS LITIGATION]."

### Topic 18: Professional Conduct
"The parties stipulate to the applicability of the Civility Principles and Code of Professional Conduct applicable in the Northern District of California and commit to civil and professional conduct throughout this litigation."

### Topic 19: Other Matters
"[IDENTIFY ANY OTHER MATTERS OF IMPORTANCE OR CONCERN FOR THE COURT'S MANAGEMENT OF THIS CASE]."

## Custom Topics (Derived from Complaint/Judge Requirements)

### Topic 1: Jurisdiction, Service, and Venue (SPLIT)

**Plaintiffs' Position:**
- State complete case caption and case number (with judge's initials per Civil L.R. 3-4)
- Confirm service: "Summons and Complaint have been served on [DEFENDANT(S)]." (Include date(s) of service with ECF numbers if available.)
- Jurisdiction: Cite the complaint's jurisdictional allegations. If federal question (28 U.S.C. § 1331), cite the statute and claim. If diversity (28 U.S.C. § 1332), state amount in controversy and citizenship. If CAFA (28 U.S.C. § 1332(d)), identify as such.
- Venue: Cite complaint's venue basis(es) per 28 U.S.C. § 1391 (e.g., "Venue is proper in this District under 28 U.S.C. § 1391(b) because: [EVENTS GIVING RISE TO CLAIM OCCURRED IN THIS DISTRICT]").
- Personal Jurisdiction: Brief explanation if alleged (e.g., "Defendants are subject to personal jurisdiction in this District because [SPECIFIC FACTS]").

**Defendants' Position:**
"[DEFENDANTS' POSITION ON JURISDICTION, SERVICE, VENUE, AND PERSONAL JURISDICTION]."

### Topic 11: Relief (SPLIT)

Extract directly from complaint's Prayer for Relief. Use singular/plural as appropriate (e.g., "Plaintiff seeks damages in an amount to be proven at trial" vs. "Plaintiffs seek damages in amounts to be proven at trial").

**Plaintiffs' Position:**
"[EXACT LANGUAGE FROM COMPLAINT'S PRAYER FOR RELIEF, ADAPTED AS NEEDED]."

**Defendants' Position:**
"[DEFENDANTS' POSITION ON RELIEF SOUGHT]."

## Formatting Baseline

### Font and Spacing
- **Font:** 12pt Times New Roman throughout (except footer: 10pt)
- **Line spacing:** Exact 24pt (not "double" or "proportional")
- **Paragraph spacing:** 0pt before, 0pt after
- **Indentation:** 0.5″ first-line indent only (except captions, tables, signature blocks)
- **Justification:** Justified alignment for body paragraphs
- **ContextualSpacing:** Enabled on all paragraphs

### Quotation Marks
- **Always use curly (typographic) quotes:** '' (single) and "" (double)
- **Never use straight quotes:** ' or "
- Applied to all quotations, placeholder text, and cited language

### Spacing After Punctuation
- **Sentence-ending periods:** Two spaces after (e.g., "...first sentence.  Second sentence.")
- **Mid-sentence abbreviations and citations:** One space (e.g., "Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(a) requires...")
- **After section/paragraph symbols (¶, §):** Nonbreaking space (e.g., "Compl. ¶ 22" not "Compl.¶22")

### Bluebook Citation Formatting (Federal Style)
- **Abbreviate "Complaint" as "Compl."** (not "Complaint"); no comma before pincite
- **Use en dash for ranges:** "¶¶ 22–44" (not hyphen)
- **Italicize introductory signals:** *See*, *Accord*, *But see*, *Contra*
- **Nonbreaking space after section/paragraph symbols:** "§ 1391" (not "§1391")
- Examples:
  - "Compl. ¶ 9" (singular)
  - "Compl. ¶¶ 22–44" (range)
  - "*See* Compl. ¶ 8" (with signal)
  - "28 U.S.C. § 1331" (statute)

### Singular/Plural Rules
- **Plaintiff (singular) / Plaintiffs (plural):** Match case caption
- **Defendant (singular) / Defendants (plural):** Match case caption
- Update in every topic and standard language block accordingly

### Judge's Initials in Case Number
- **Location:** Civil L.R. 3-4 requires judge's initials in the case number
- **Format:** "4:26-cv-05924-ABC" (where ABC are judge's initials)
- **Examples:**
  - "4:26-cv-05924-AMO" → Judge Martínez-Olguín (initials: A.M.O. or similar per court convention)
  - "4:26-cv-05924-JD" → Judge Davila

Look up judge's initials on the N.D. Cal. USCOURTS website or Rule 16 Order.

### Caption Format
The caption box typically appears on the first page with:
- Party names (all caps) with "Plaintiff(s)" and "Defendant(s)" designations
- Case number with judge initials
- Assigned judge's name and title
- Document title: "JOINT CASE MANAGEMENT STATEMENT"
- CMC hearing date, time, and courtroom (from Rule 16 Order or placeholders)

### Footer Format
- **Content:** Document title (left-aligned) and case number with judge initials (left-aligned)
- **Font:** 10pt Times New Roman
- **Page numbering:** No number on caption page (first page); start numbering at 1 on second page

### Civil L.R. 16-9(b) Class Action Supplement
If the case is a putative class action, confirm the CMS also addresses:
1. **Class definition:** Precise statement of class definition and any subclasses
2. **Numerosity:** Estimate of class member count
3. **Commonality:** Common questions of law or fact
4. **Typicality:** Typicality of claims/defenses
5. **Adequacy:** Adequacy of class representative and counsel
6. **Superiority:** Why class action is superior to individual suits
7. **Damages calculation methodology:** If applicable
8. **Injunctive or declaratory relief:** If applicable

## Topic-Specific Drafting Notes

### Topic 1 (Jurisdiction, Service, Venue)
- Always source jurisdiction allegations directly from complaint
- If CAFA, identify explicitly: "This action arises under the Class Action Fairness Act, 28 U.S.C. § 1332(d)."
- Verify service: Search PACER or docket for returned Summons with ECF number and date
- Venue: Cite specific subsection of 28 U.S.C. § 1391; explain connection to this district
- Flag any non-standard jurisdictional bases (e.g., supplemental jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1367) for double-check

### Topic 3 (Factual Background)
Split into Plaintiffs' and Defendants' positions. Factual statements are neutral but parties may emphasize different facts or context. No legal conclusions in factual sections.

### Topic 4 (Legal Theories)
Plaintiffs' theories should track complaint's causes of action. Defendants' theories typically introduce affirmative defenses or alternative theories.

### Topic 5 (Relief Sought)
Usually joint; state relief objectively (damages category, injunctive relief, etc.). Avoid advocacy.

### Topic 11 (Relief — Detailed)
Split into Plaintiffs' and Defendants' positions. Plaintiffs' position mirrors Prayer for Relief from complaint. Defendants' position may assert counterclaims or alternative relief theories.

### Topic 15 (Scheduling)
- Class-cert motion date: Midpoint of 8–10 month range after CMC hearing (typically 9 months, FRCP-6-adjusted)
- Opposition deadline: 45–60 days after motion filing (subject to judge-specific notice periods)
- Reply deadline: 30–45 days after opposition filing
- Hearing notice: 35 calendar days per Civil L.R. 7-2(a), unless judge's standing order specifies otherwise
- All dates computed using `scripts/frcp6_date.py` with FRCP 6(a) roll-forward for weekends/holidays
